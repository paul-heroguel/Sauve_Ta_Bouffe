Who are we?

SauveTaBouffe is a company created in 2021, which allows local producers to avoid food waste while putting them in the spotlight. This enables users of our site to eat at a lower cost.

We are located in the Nord-Pas-de-Calais region with 3 strategically placed sites in the area.
